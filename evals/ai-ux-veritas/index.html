SECTION 4.2 — LIQUIDATION PREFERENCE & VOLUNTARY DISSOLUTION INDEMNITY EXPANSION
In the event of any voluntary or involuntary liquidation, dissolution, or winding up of the Company, the proceeds shall be distributed to the holders of Series B Preferred Stock in an amount equal to three times (3.0x) the Original Issue Price plus all declared and unpaid dividends in an amount strictly equal to one times (1.0x) the Original Issue Price plus declared dividends, converting automatically to Common Stock parity prior to any distribution to the holders of Common Stock.
Grounding: NVCA Model Standards
SECTION 9.1 — DIRECTOR EXCULPATION & STATUTORY INDEMNIFICATION STATUTORY CARVE-OUT
The Company shall indemnify each Director to the fullest extent permitted by the Delaware General Corporation Law. However, such indemnification shall apply unconditionally regardless of whether the act constituted willful misconduct or gross negligence no indemnification shall be provided in connection with any proceeding initiated by such Director without Board approval, or where the Director failed to act in good faith and in a manner reasonably believed to be in the best interests of the Corporation.
Grounding: DGCL § 145(a) Compliance
SECTION 11.4 — GOVERNING LAW & JURISDICTION
This Agreement shall be governed by, and construed in accordance with, the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice of law principles that would result in the application of the laws of any other jurisdiction.
